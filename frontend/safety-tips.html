Cyber Safety Tips

Simple habits that can help you stay safer online.

🔐

Use Strong Passwords

Use long and unique passwords. Avoid using easily guessed information.

🎣

Beware of Phishing

Verify suspicious emails, messages and links before sharing any personal information.

🛡️

Enable 2FA

Turn on two-factor authentication whenever it is available.

📱

Keep Devices Updated

Install security updates for your operating system and applications.

📶

Be Careful on Public Wi-Fi

Avoid accessing sensitive accounts on untrusted public networks.

🚫

Don't Share Passwords

Never share your passwords or verification codes with other people.
